Design notes

The chat is the whole page. overeem.io's yellow banner is the header; the conversation below it follows the modern chat pattern. All answers in this prototype are sample text; anything in [brackets] would come from the real CV.

Decisions

  • Language and theme switches sit top-right in the header, on a dark see-through pill so they read on both the yellow and the photo. Both are radio groups: the checked option is the one tab stop, and arrow keys move between options.
  • "NL | EN" switches in place on one URL, as in the spec. A click is remembered in localStorage; without one the page follows the browser's languages, falling back to English. Switching keeps the conversation: the UI copy changes, earlier answers stay in their language, and new answers follow the new language.
  • Theme: Licht, Donker and Automatisch, in the spec's order, with Automatisch as the default. The choice is remembered in localStorage; @nuxtjs/color-mode applies it before the first paint, so dark-mode visitors don't see a white flash.
  • Everything placed on the brand yellow is white, as on overeem.io: the header text, the send button arrow and the privacy note's title bar. This is a deliberate exception to the spec's WCAG AA rule. White on #F7B943 is 1.75:1, against 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and 3:1 for icons. White would reach 3:1 on #bc8d33 and 4.5:1 on #957029; dark slate (#22303a) on the current yellow is 7.7:1.
  • Answers have no bubble. They're plain text with white-space: pre-wrap, so the model's line breaks (like the meeting summary) survive without Markdown, and they're rendered with text interpolation, never v-html. Only the visitor's own messages get a taupe bubble.
  • The header is the overeem.io banner at exactly the site's sizes: 24rem tall, 32rem from 992px, side by side from 576px and stacked below that, with the same type sizes per breakpoint.
  • Every state maps to the API contract. meeting_sent shows a green confirmation line that stays even if the stream then errors. An error event shows a retry button and keeps the partial answer out of the history. A 429 shows the "Even rustig aan" message. A cut-off answer gets a note.
  • Input field: Enter sends and Shift+Enter adds a new line. The character counter appears from 900 characters and turns red past 1000, the server-side limit. Sending is disabled while an answer is streaming. "Nieuw gesprek" sits with the input, right of the AI notice, because it acts on the conversation; it only appears once there is a conversation. Clicking it clears the conversation and scrolls to the end of the page, just like sending.
  • The input is never pinned: at every width it sits in the normal page flow, right after the last message, with the footer below it. When the conversation is too short to fill the screen, the chat area stretches (the page, footer included, is at least one screen tall), so the input and footer rest at the bottom of the screen instead of halfway up. The header scrolls away as the conversation grows. On mobile the suggestions stack into one column and the switches move above the name.
  • Footer: overeem.io's taupe contact block returns as the footer, with the email address and LinkedIn, at the end of the page in every state. Dark text on the taupe is 6.35:1 (white would be 2.30:1), and like the yellow header it keeps its colours in dark mode. The error and rate-limit messages point to it ("via e-mail of LinkedIn onderaan de pagina"), so a visitor never leaves with nothing.
  • Auto-scroll ("stick to bottom"): sending, and "Nieuw gesprek", scroll the page to its end, so the input sits just above the footer at the bottom of the screen. Any scroll you make yourself (wheel or trackpad up, touch, Page Up / arrow up / Home outside the input, dragging the scrollbar) turns following off at once, so the stream never fights you. Scrolling back to the very bottom turns it on again. Switching language or theme never scrolls.
